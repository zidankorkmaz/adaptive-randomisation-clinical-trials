# Fixed vs Adaptive Randomisation in Clinical Trials

Final-year project, University of Southampton · **83%, 1st of 238 students**

A Monte Carlo simulation study in R (48,000+ trials) comparing fixed and Bayesian response-adaptive randomisation (RAR) in two-arm clinical trials, measuring Type I error, power, and ethical allocation.

## Key findings
- Bayesian RAR assigned **83%** of patients to the better treatment (vs 50% fixed), giving ~10 more successes per 100 patients
- This came at a cost of up to **15 percentage points** in power
- Adaptive gains **do not fully carry over** to cluster-randomised trials
